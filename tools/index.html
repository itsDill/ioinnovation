How it works

From two filings to a useful comparison

The tool organizes reported positions; the SEC filings remain the source of record.

01

Choose two institutions

Select any two managers in the current coverage set. Review the report period and filing date for each, and open the linked SEC source.

02

Find overlap and differences

Filter to shared positions or positions unique to one filing. Search by issuer, ticker, or CUSIP, and compare reported values and portfolio weights.

03

Verify and continue research

Check the original filings, export the filtered table to CSV, and use the comparison as a starting point for your own analysis.

Form 13F filings are delayed snapshots of certain reportable securities—not real-time portfolios or a complete view of an institution’s investments. When report periods differ, the filings are not a like-for-like comparison. This tool is for informational research, not investment advice. Read the data methodology.
