Important assumptions and disclaimer

This educational calculator is not investment, tax, or financial advice. Results are hypothetical and are not guaranteed. It assumes a constant annual return compounded monthly, regular contributions at the selected timing, and no taxes, fees, or changes to contributions. Actual returns vary, may be negative, and investment values can fall. Inflation-adjusted figures are approximations. Verify assumptions and consider seeking advice from a qualified professional.
