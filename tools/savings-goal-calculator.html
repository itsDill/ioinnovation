Important assumptions and disclaimer

This educational estimate is not financial or investment advice. It assumes a constant annual interest rate converted to a monthly equivalent and regular deposits at the selected time of month. Actual rates may change, fees and taxes are excluded, and the estimate does not guarantee that a goal will be reached. A negative interest assumption models a declining balance. Currency selection changes formatting only and does not convert values. Verify assumptions and consider consulting a qualified professional.
