Important assumptions and disclaimer

This educational calculation is not investment, tax, or financial advice. It assumes the entered prices and cumulative per-share cash dividends apply to the same holding and currency. Total return is calculated as ending share value plus dividends per share multiplied by the share quantity, compared with the starting value. Dividends are not reinvested; fees, taxes, additional purchases, splits, and other cash flows are excluded. Annualized return is shown only when valid dates are entered and assumes a single start and end value. Past performance does not predict future results. Currency selection changes formatting only; no exchange conversion is performed.
