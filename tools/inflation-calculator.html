Important assumptions and disclaimer

This educational calculator is not financial advice and does not use observed historical or official CPI data. It applies the selected constant annual rate, compounded once per year, to illustrate how prices or purchasing power might change. Actual inflation varies across time, regions, and categories; your personal cost of living may differ. Currency changes the display symbol only; no foreign-exchange conversion is performed.
