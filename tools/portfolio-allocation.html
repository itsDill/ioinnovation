Important assumptions and disclaimer

This educational planner is not investment, tax, or financial advice. It compares entered values with hypothetical target weights and does not account for fees, taxes, liquidity, risk tolerance, investment goals, or asset correlations. The contribution view assumes all new cash can be allocated according to target weights and does not recommend trades or sales. Review allocations in the context of your circumstances and consider consulting a qualified professional.
