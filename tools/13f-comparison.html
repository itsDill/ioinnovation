How to read this comparison

  • Form 13F reports certain U.S. securities as of a past quarter-end; it is not a real-time portfolio or a complete view of an institution’s investments.
  • Filing dates and report periods can differ. When periods do not match, compare each side as a separate snapshot—not as a direct performance or trade comparison.
  • Matching uses CUSIP and reported option type where available. Values are the amounts disclosed in each filing; they are not current market values.
  • This tool is for informational research, not investment advice. Confirm details in the linked SEC filing.
