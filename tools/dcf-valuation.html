Important assumptions and disclaimer

This simplified discounted cash flow model is educational, not investment, tax, or financial advice. It assumes a constant annual FCFF growth rate during the forecast, a Gordon growth terminal value, and that the discount rate exceeds perpetual growth. It does not model taxes, changing margins, dilution, options, minority interests, or other balance-sheet adjustments. Actual results and market prices may differ materially. A DCF is highly sensitive to its assumptions; verify inputs and consider consulting a qualified professional.
