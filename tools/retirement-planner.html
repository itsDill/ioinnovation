Important assumptions and disclaimer

This educational scenario is not investment, tax, or retirement advice. It assumes a constant annual return compounded monthly, fixed monthly contributions made at month end, and a constant inflation rate. Actual returns vary and can be negative; fees, taxes, contribution changes, benefit income, longevity, and sequence-of-returns risk are not modeled. The withdrawal rate is an illustrative planning assumption, not a guaranteed sustainable rate. Retirement income and target values are estimates, not promises. Currency changes formatting only. Consider consulting a qualified professional.
