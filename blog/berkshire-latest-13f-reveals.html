What Berkshire's latest 13F reveals

Published: 2026-08-18 • Author: IO Innovation

A 13F is a lagged snapshot, not a live trade feed. It still helps identify concentration and directional changes in large public equity positions.

What to review first

  • Position concentration: does one stock dominate portfolio risk?
  • Net adds or trims: did conviction rise or fade?
  • New entries and exits: what changed in the opportunity set?

How to avoid common mistakes

  • Do not assume the position is unchanged today; 13F is delayed.
  • Do not infer motive from one quarter; use a multi-quarter sequence.
  • Cross-check with Form 4 and earnings commentary for fresher context.
