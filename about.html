About

Practical tools for financial research and planning

IO Innovation is an independent project building accessible tools for investing, company research, and personal finance. Contact: contact@ioinnovationfund.com.

The collection

The site brings together practical calculators and research helpers, including a dividend calculator and a guided 10-K review tool. Some tools are still previews and will become interactive over time.

Using the tools

  • Calculator outputs are estimates based on the values and assumptions entered.
  • Review assumptions and verify important information with authoritative sources.
  • The collection is educational and does not replace independent research or professional advice.

Suggestions and feedback

Have an idea for a useful tool or a suggestion for an existing one? Get in touch and share your feedback.
