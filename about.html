Global asset intelligence

IO Innovation Fund is an independent research and information platform focused on physical assets, financial assets, global resources, and market intelligence. Contact: contact@ioinnovationfund.com.

The collection

Our research framework connects infrastructure and industrial assets with company ownership, institutional holdings, resource supply chains, and investment themes. Coverage is being developed in stages; current tools and data are identified on their respective pages.

Research principles
