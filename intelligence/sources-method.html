Research method

Make evidence and uncertainty inspectable

Responsible research documents where information came from, what period it covers, what assumptions were added, and which questions remain unresolved. No data system removes ambiguity or guarantees completeness.

01 / PROVENANCE

Source quality

Prefer traceable original materials and record source type, date, and relevant scope.

02 / DEFINITIONS

Comparability

Document units, entity definitions, reporting periods, and any transformations.

03 / INTERPRETATION

Uncertainty

Label estimates, inference, missing information, and plausible alternative readings.
