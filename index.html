A quick guide

How to use the 13F comparison

Start with two tracked filers. The comparison does the organizing; the SEC filings remain the source of record.

01

Choose two institutions

Select any two managers in the dataset. Each selection shows its report period, filing date, and direct SEC source link.

02

Explore the overlap

Filter to shared positions or see what appears in only one filing. Search by issuer, ticker, or CUSIP and compare reported values and portfolio weights.

03

Verify and take it with you

Check each filing’s reporting period, open the source documents, or export your filtered comparison as a CSV.

13F filings are delayed snapshots of certain reportable securities—not real-time portfolios or a complete view of an institution’s investments. Different report periods are not directly comparable.
